## English version

Subject: Fan-made battle planning tool for Whiteout Survival — respectful
inquiry about permission and possible cooperation

Dear Century Games team,

My name is Martin Ling. I am a long-time Whiteout Survival player based in
Hong Kong, and I write to you as a fan first.

Over the past months I have built a small web tool, WOSTests.com, that helps
players — especially alliance rally leaders — estimate the likely outcome of
a rally or garrison battle before they commit troops to it. Players enter
their own troop and hero information by hand (or upload their own battle
screenshots), and the tool returns a probability estimate with an honest
confidence label. It is deliberately conservative: when a fight is too close
to call, it says so.

I want to be straightforward about how the tool relates to your IP:

- It contains **no artwork, icons, or assets from Whiteout Survival** — all
  visuals are our own original creations.
- It uses the game's name only descriptively ("a tool for Whiteout Survival
  players"), with a clear notice that it is fan-made and not affiliated with
  or endorsed by Century Games.
- It does not interact with your game servers or client in any way, does not
  automate gameplay, and never asks players for their game credentials.
- Its predictions are built from players' own battle reports, entered
  voluntarily by them.

I am writing for two reasons. First, out of respect: if any aspect of the
tool concerns your team, I would genuinely like to know and will address it.
Second, with hope: if Century Games ever considers working with community
tools — for example an approved fan-tool programme, official data access, or
simply guidelines you would like tools like mine to follow — I would be glad
to participate and to adapt the tool to your requirements.

Whiteout Survival's alliance play is what makes this community special, and
my only aim is to help players make better decisions inside the game you
built. Thank you for reading, and for the game itself.

Respectfully,

Martin Ling
[contact email]
WOSTests.com

## 中文版本（简体）

主题：《寒霜启示录》(Whiteout Survival) 玩家自制战斗规划工具 —— 关于授权与合作可能性的诚恳咨询

尊敬的 Century Games（点点互动）团队：

您好！我叫 Martin Ling，是一名居住在香港的《Whiteout Survival》长期玩家。首先，
我是以一名玩家和粉丝的身份给您写这封信。

在过去几个月里，我开发了一个小型网页工具 WOSTests.com，帮助玩家——尤其是联盟中
负责发起集结的指挥官——在投入部队之前，估算集结战或驻防战的可能结果。玩家自行
手动输入自己的部队和英雄信息（或上传自己的战报截图），工具会给出一个概率估算，
并附上诚实的置信度说明。这个工具刻意保持保守：当一场战斗胜负难料时，它会如实
说明"难以判断"。

关于本工具与贵司知识产权的关系，我希望坦诚说明：

- 工具中**不包含任何来自《Whiteout Survival》的美术、图标或素材**——所有视觉
  内容均为我们自己的原创设计。
- 仅在描述性意义上使用游戏名称（"面向 Whiteout Survival 玩家的工具"），并明确
  标注这是玩家自制工具，与 Century Games 无关联、亦未获其认可。
- 工具不以任何方式接触贵司的游戏服务器或客户端，不自动化任何游戏操作，也绝不
  要求玩家提供游戏账号密码。
- 所有预测均基于玩家自愿输入的、属于他们自己的战报数据。

我写这封信有两个目的。其一是出于尊重：如果工具的任何方面令贵司担忧，我真诚希望
了解，并将积极整改。其二是出于期待：如果 Century Games 未来考虑与社区工具合作——
例如认证粉丝工具计划、官方数据接口，或仅仅是希望此类工具遵循的规范指引——我都
非常乐意参与，并按贵司要求调整工具。

《Whiteout Survival》的联盟玩法是这个社区最珍贵的部分，我唯一的目标，是帮助玩家
在你们创造的游戏中做出更好的决策。感谢您抽空阅读，也感谢你们带来这款游戏。

此致
敬礼

Martin Ling

WOSTests.com
