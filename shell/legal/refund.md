## First subscription: 7-day refund window

If this is your **first** paid subscription to WOSTests.com and you are not
happy with it, email [support email TBD] within **7 days** of the first
charge and we will refund that charge in full and cancel the subscription.
No questions required (though feedback helps us).

## Renewals

Renewal charges are generally **not refundable**, because you keep access
for the whole period you paid for and can cancel at any time before renewal.
If you forgot to cancel and contact us within 72 hours of a renewal charge,
with no meaningful usage since the charge, we will normally refund it as a
goodwill gesture — this is discretionary, not a right.

## How refunds are paid

Refunds go back through Stripe to the original payment method, usually
within 5–10 business days. We never handle your card details directly.

## What refunds do not cover

- Charges older than the windows above.
- Accounts terminated for a ToS §6 breach (reverse engineering, bulk
  querying, quota circumvention) — see the Terms of Service.
- Dissatisfaction with an in-game outcome. Predictions are probabilistic
  estimates and say so, including their own confidence labels; a battle
  going the unlikely way is the model working as described, not a defect.

## Statutory rights

Nothing here limits any consumer right you have under the law of your place
of residence that cannot be waived by agreement. If such a right gives you
more than this policy does, the law wins.

## Chargebacks

Please contact us before opening a card dispute — we resolve almost
everything faster than a chargeback does. Accounts with chargebacks on
legitimately delivered service may be suspended while we sort it out.
