## 1. What Experts are

Experts are a separate passive-bonus system from Heroes: 10 named characters
(as of 2026-08-05), each with a static portrait, a short backstory, **4
numbered Skills**, **1 Talent**, and a **Level 1-100 "Affinity" track** that
grants its own Troops' Lethality/Health bonus independent of the 5 skills.

**Unlocking:** the system opens at **Furnace Level 25**
(source: [Bluestacks](https://www.bluestacks.com/blog/game-guides/white-out-survival/wos-experts-guide-en.html)).
Players meet Experts through the **Tundra Trek** / **Frontier Trek** event
lines and raise **Affinity** with them by completing treks or gifting items;
[Out of Games](https://outof.games/realms/whiteoutsurvival/guides/497-what-are-experts-in-whiteout-survival/)
states an expert "join[s] your City" once Affinity reaches **1,000** (not
corroborated by a second source — treat as approximate).

**Leveling (two independent tracks, both drawn from the same "Affinity" pool):**
1. **Expert Level** (aka "Affinity Level" on-wiki, "Relationship Level" in
   community guides), **1-100.** Consumes Affinity points; every 10 levels is a
   "Relationship Advancement" threshold that additionally costs **Sigils**
   (common or expert-specific) — Out of Games: *"Each Expert has 100
   Relationship Levels divided by 10 thresholds for Relationship
   Advancement."* Each Expert-Level step grants a small, uniform **Troops'
   Lethality + Health %** bonus (tabulated per-expert in §2/§4), on top of and
   separate from the 4 skills.
2. **Individual skill levels** (4 skills, each 1-10 or 1-20, + 1 Talent, 1-11).
   Leveled in the **Dawn Academy** using **Books of Knowledge** (+ skill EXP);
   each skill's cap rises with Expert Level. Per aggregated secondary guides
   (not verified against one primary page): Skill 1 unlocks around
   Relationship Lv.10, Skill 2 ~Lv.20, Skill 3 ~Lv.30, Skill 4 ~Lv.40 — **treat
   thresholds as approximate/UNKNOWN**; only the ordering is well corroborated.

**Skill types — economy/development vs combat/war:** every expert we sampled
follows the same shape: **3 of the 4 numbered skills are non-combat economy/
utility** (troop recruitment, chest loot, resource-per-minute, mission
refreshes, construction speed, alliance-shop discounts, etc.), and **the
skill(s) that grant Attack/Defense/Lethality/Health/Rally-Capacity are the
combat/war skills.** Whether a combat skill is *usable in normal PvP* splits
experts into two groups:
- **Unconditional (always-on) combat skills** — Romulus, Gareth-3: the effect
  text carries no scope qualifier ("Troops' Attack and Defense +X%"), so the
  bonus applies to every battle the account fights, attack or defense, rally
  or garrison, PvP or PvE.
- **Context-gated combat skills** — Valeria ("...during State of Power's
  Battle Phase"), Fabian ("...Foundry Battle and Tundra Hellfire"), and every
  event-specific expert (Kathy/Frostfire Mine, Ronne/Trade Route,
  Holger/Arena, Cyrille/Bear Hunt, Baldur/Alliance events, Agnes has no
  combat-stat skill at all): the bonus text explicitly names the one
  mini-game/event it fires in, and (per Out of Games, re: Fabian) is
  *"practically useless"* outside that context.

See §3 for how this maps onto the battle-report stat panel.

## 3. Which experts appear in battle-report stat panels

**Owner statement (ground truth, Martin, 2026-08):** normally **Romulus** and
**Gareth-3** appear in the Stat Bonuses panel; during **SvS** (State of Power),
**Valeria** appears; during **Foundry**, **Fabian** appears.

**Why — from the wiki's own effect text (§2.1-2.4):**

| Expert | Combat-skill scope text | Implication |
|---|---|---|
| Romulus | "Troops' Attack and Defense..." / "Troops' Lethality and Health..." — **no scope qualifier** | Unconditional global modifier → always contributes to the Stat Bonuses screen, any battle type, attack or defense |
| Gareth-3 | "Troops' Defense..." / "Troops' Health..." / "Enemy Troop's Lethality..." — **no scope qualifier** | Same — always active |
| Valeria | "...during **State of Power's Battle Phase**" (×3 of 5 abilities) | Textually inert outside SoP's Battle Phase → only shows up in an SvS battle report |
| Fabian | "...**Foundry Battle and Tundra Hellfire**" (×4 of 5 abilities) | Textually inert outside those two modes → only shows up in a Foundry/Tundra-Hellfire report |

This is a **content-text distinction, not a separate "equip slot" mechanic** —
no source found (wiki or secondary guides) states a fixed number of "Expert
slots" or a formal Attack-expert/Defense-expert split (see §5, UNKNOWN). The
simplest reading supported by the evidence: every expert's bonuses are always
"equipped," but each skill's own effect text scopes *when* it applies; Romulus
and Gareth-3 happen to have zero scoping on their combat skills, so they read
as the default always-on PvP pair, while Valeria/Fabian (and every brief
expert in §2.5) are hard-scoped to one event.

## 4. Worked totals for our reference account

### Method note — determining which of the 5 input numbers is which skill

The task gives 5 skill-level numbers per account, in on-screen left-to-right
order, without saying which of the 5 icons (4 Skills + Talent) each belongs
to. This is **resolved by arithmetic, not assumed.** Skill max levels are
consistent across both experts: Skill 1 caps at **10**, Skills 2-4 cap at
**20** each, Talent caps at **11**.

**Romulus, `[11, 10, 20, 20, 20]`:** `11` exceeds Skill 1's cap (10) and every
cap except the Talent's (11) and Skills 2-4's (20) — but if `11` took a
Skill-2/3/4 slot, the Talent would be forced onto a `20`, exceeding its own
cap of 11. The only consistent assignment: **Talent = 11 (maxed), Skill 1 = 10
(maxed), Skills 2/3/4 = 20/20/20 (maxed)** — this Romulus is **fully maxed**,
unsurprising at Expert Level 100 (also the cap). Forced deduction, not a guess.

**Gareth-3, `[9, 10, 7, 6, 3]`:** every value here is ≤ every skill's cap, so
max-level logic alone doesn't force an order. Applying the **same slot
template** used above (Talent, Skill 1, Skill 2, Skill 3, Skill 4, in that
left-to-right position) gives Talent=9, Skill1=10, Skill2=7, Skill3=6,
**Skill4 (Fearsome Reputation) = 3**. That last figure independently
reproduces the battle report's quoted **"Enemy Lethality Penalty (Expert
Skill) −0.75%"** exactly (Fearsome Reputation's own table: Lv.3 = 0.75%, see
§2.2) — two independent constraints (Romulus's forced ordering, and the
−0.75% clue) both point at the same slot template, which is the strongest
corroboration available without an in-game screenshot. Treat the *order* as a
well-evidenced deduction, not a wiki-sourced fact.
