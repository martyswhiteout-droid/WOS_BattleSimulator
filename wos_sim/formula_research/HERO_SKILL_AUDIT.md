# Hero Skill Codification Audit — for Martin's review

> **STATUS: HISTORICAL PROVENANCE, not the current acceptance oracle (per QA 2026-07-25).**
> This is the *original* audit of the OLD flat codification. The corrected behaviour now
> lives in `hero_roster.py` / `hero_kit.py`; use `HERO_TEST_CASES.md` as the current
> reference. Three original interpretations here are themselves superseded:
> - **Gordon S1** (below): said poison affects all enemy classes — WRONG. "Apply poison to
>   the target" = only the struck target unit (`Target.TARGET`), Martin-confirmed.
> - **Renee S3**: the "all troops" is the attacking *receiver*, not the enemy *victim*
>   (the marked target) — the original finding conflated them.
> - **Gwen S2**: grouped as an extra-attack, but "deal extra damage" is a damage boost,
>   not "launch an extra attack" — it is correctly a `DamageMod` in the roster.

**Method:** all 54 heroes' codified skill rows compared against their wiki description text (LLM audit, wiki text as ground truth). Unflagged skills judged faithful. Non-combat skills (research/training/march/building speed) are correctly omitted from the engine and NOT flagged.

**34 flagged — 11 WRONG, 23 SUSPECT.**  By category: expressibility 8, attribute 6, cadence 5, missing_clause 5, magnitude 4, receiver 4, target 2

## Systemic patterns (the important part for the per-hero-class rebuild)

1. **Dropped combat skills** — 4 real combat skills have NO codified rows at all: Bahiti S2, Jessie S2, Patrick S2, Sergey S2 (all simple all-troop buffs/debuffs). A systematic ingestion gap, not one-offs.
2. **Shields coded as +Defense** — Gatot S2 and Elif S3 are Attack-scaled Shields (absorb pools) codified as flat +30% Defense. The data model cannot express a shield; this is why the law needed a bespoke Gatot budget. Every shield hero needs a real absorb mechanic.
3. **'more/extra damage' coded as the Attack stat** — Cara S2, Mia S2, Rufus S2, Sonya S2: 'deal X% more damage' became +X% Attack instead of +X% Damage Dealt (different scaling in the law).
4. **Extra-attack / Reap / stun modeled as a damage buff** — Freya S2, Reina S3, Wayne S1, Gwen S2, Sonya S3: added strikes and crowd-control are flattened into Damage-Dealt procs.
5. **'dealing 200% damage' doubled** — Hector S3, Philly S2, Reina S3 store per_proc +2.0 (+200% extra); '200% damage' most likely means 2x normal = +100% extra. Systematic magnitude doubling on chance procs.
6. **'target' coded as enemy Infantry** — Ahmose S3, Ligeia S2/S3, Xura S2: 'the target's damage taken' applied to enemy Infantry specifically rather than the attacked Target.
7. **Passive coded as a proc (or vice-versa)** — Flint S1, Logan S1, Lynn S1: mechanic (Stats-based vs Turn-based vs Chance-based) inconsistent with siblings of identical wording.
8. **Missing enemy class in a multi-class debuff** — Gordon S1 (enemy Infantry row duplicated, enemy Lancer missing), Renee S3 (enemy Lancer missing).
9. **Vulcanus S2 — code matches the WIKI but not the MEASUREMENT.** Codified +100%/every-5-attacks; the project measured +20%/every-6 (the law's x31/30 fold). Highest-priority reconciliation.

> Note on the `amount` field: for proc skills the model stores an EV-averaged `amount` alongside the raw `amount_per_proc`. The new deterministic proc layer should use the RAW value + real cadence, never the EV-average (that averaging is itself a fudge). One case (Lloyd S3) even stores the raw where its siblings store the EV.
